Challenge  – Cyclone Detection & Early Warning System
Domain: Disaster Management & Climate Resilience
The Challenge
Coastal regions of India, particularly areas along the Bay of Bengal and the Arabian Sea, are vulnerable to cyclones, severe storms, heavy rainfall, strong winds, and storm surges. Delayed identification of cyclone formation, changes in storm intensity, and limited access to timely warnings can put human lives, infrastructure, agriculture, and coastal communities at risk.
There is a need for an intelligent, AI-powered system that can analyze weather patterns, detect potential cyclone formations, predict cyclone movement and intensity, and provide timely early warnings to help authorities and communities prepare for potential disasters.
Objective
Develop an Agentic AI-based Cyclone Detection & Early Warning System that analyzes meteorological and satellite data, identifies potential cyclone formations, predicts cyclone trajectories and intensity, assesses potential impact zones, and generates timely alerts and preparedness recommendations for vulnerable regions.
Suggested AI Agents
- Weather Data Analysis Agent – Analyze meteorological data, including atmospheric pressure, wind speed, humidity, rainfall, and sea surface temperature.
- Cyclone Detection Agent – Identify potential cyclone formations by analyzing satellite imagery and changing weather patterns.
- Cyclone Tracking & Prediction Agent – Predict cyclone movement, direction, speed, and possible landfall locations using historical and real-time weather data.
- Cyclone Intensity Prediction Agent – Estimate potential cyclone intensity and identify the possibility of rapid intensification.
- Impact Assessment Agent – Identify vulnerable coastal regions and assess potential risks to population, infrastructure, agriculture, and essential services.
- Early Warning & Alert Agent – Generate location-specific alerts and preparedness recommendations for communities and disaster management authorities.
- Disaster Response Recommendation Agent – Suggest evacuation preparedness, emergency resource allocation, shelter identification, and response strategies.
Expected Outcome
The solution should help disaster management authorities, coastal communities, government agencies, and emergency response teams identify potential cyclone threats, monitor cyclone movement, understand possible impact zones, and make informed decisions through timely warnings and actionable preparedness recommendations.
