Bengaluru Hackathon 2026 – Build Agentic AI Solutions with IBM Bob 
Guidelines & Requirements-  
1. Event Overview 
The IBM Hackathon is a competitive innovation event where teams leverage IBM Bob, IBM 
Granite models and IBM Cloud services to design, build, and deploy cloud-based solutions. 
The objective is to solve real-world problem statements efficiently while demonstrating cloud 
computing, Agentic AI, Generative AI and Machine Learning. 
How can agentic AI technology improve the productivity of an important everyday activity? 
Identify a real-world business process or task and present your innovative proof of concept 
for how that activity could be streamlined using IBM’s watsonx.ai generative AI solution. IBM, 
a pioneer in the tech industry, has been at the forefront of innovation for decades. Their 
contributions span across various fields, including AI, cloud computing, and quantum 
computing. IBM's cutting-edge technology and research continue to drive advancements in 
multiple sectors. 
2. General Guidelines 
• 
Participants will register in teams of up to 3 members, with each team representing a 
college. 
• 
Participants must bring their own laptops with all required tools and software pre-
installed, along with their own internet connection/data. Internet connectivity will not 
be provided by the organizers. 
• 
The hackathon will have a fixed duration of 180 minutes, and all submissions must 
be completed within the given timeframe. 
• 
Each team will get 7 Minutes to present + 3 minutes Q&A with Jury. 
• 
Teams must adhere to ethical standards—plagiarism, hard-coded results, or use of 
unauthorized services is strictly prohibited. 
3.Judging criteria will include:    
Each submission will be judged on the following criteria with a maximum score of 20 points 
and the final score being the average of the evaluators’ scores: 
1)Completeness and Feasibility (5 points): 
• How feasible is the solution? 
• How fully has the idea been thought-out and planned? 
• How complete is the proof-of-concept submitted? 
• How clear is the application of IBM technology? 
2)Creativity and Innovation (5 points): 
• How unique and original was the approach in applying AI technology to address the 
stated issue? 
• Is the solution differentiated in the market?

3)Design and Usability (5 points): 
• How good is the design, user experience, and ease of use of the solution? 
• How quickly and easily could it be put to use in real-world scenarios and adopted by 
target users? 
4)Effectiveness and Efficiency (5 points): 
• Does the solution address a high priority and relevant issue within the learning lab 
theme? 
• Does it achieve its goal effectively and efficiently? 
• Can it achieve a measurable impact in the field? 
• Does it have the potential to scale to more users or use cases? 
Note- Organizers will provide problem statements on the event day. 
4. Do’s & Don’ts 
  Do’s 
• 
Ensure your team understands the problem statement thoroughly before starting. 
• 
Bring your own laptop, charger, and required accessories.  
• 
Ensure all required software, tools, accounts, and dependencies are installed and 
tested before the hackathon.  
• 
Bring your own internet connection/mobile hotspot and sufficient data.  
• 
Use IBM Bob, IBM Granite LLM, and IBM Cloud as specified for the challenge.  
• 
Use appropriate and reliable datasets for your solution.  
• 
Clearly define the problem, solution approach, AI agents, workflow, and expected 
impact.  
• 
Ensure your solution is functional, feasible, user-friendly, and scalable.  
• 
Maintain data privacy and responsible AI practices.  
• 
Keep regular backups of your code, datasets, and project files.  
• 
Document your approach, architecture diagram, and deployment steps clearly for the 
Jury. 
• 
Collaborate efficiently and divide tasks among team members.  
- One can be a team leader 
- One can work on the PPT / Collation 
• 
Be prepared to demonstrate your working prototype to the evaluation panel.  
  Don’ts 
• 
Do not depend on organizers for laptops, internet connectivity, or personal data.  
• 
Do not use unauthorized, copyrighted, confidential, or personally identifiable 
data.  
• 
Do not submit a solution that is copied from another participant/team or an 
existing project without meaningful originality.  
• 
Do not make unsupported claims about the accuracy or impact of your AI solution.  
• 
Do not share API keys, passwords, credentials, or other sensitive information.  
• 
Do not make changes to another team’s project, code, or data without permission.
